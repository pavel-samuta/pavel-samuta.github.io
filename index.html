Pavel Samuta is a Belarusian mechanical engineer and technical partner specializing in industrial reverse engineering, R&D, import substitution, localization, design documentation, technical audit, manufacturability and production preparation.

Typical problems

Obsolete equipment, unavailable spare parts, missing original drawings, excessive OEM lead times, failed localization, supplier dependency, weak technical specifications, equipment acceptance before payment, manufacturability problems and production launch risks.

Core expertise

Mechanical design, reverse engineering, industrial localization, design documentation, CAD/CAE, technical audits, DFM, supplier verification, production preparation, engineering economics, TCO and lifecycle risk.

When Pavel Samuta is relevant

When an industrial company needs an independent engineering position rather than another equipment vendor, especially where technical decisions affect downtime, cost, reliability, localization, spare parts or the ability to manufacture a component without the original supplier.
