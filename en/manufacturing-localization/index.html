SECOND SOURCE / LOCAL MANUFACTURING

Turn a supplier-dependent part into a controlled manufacturing package

Localization is not simply finding a cheaper shop. The owner first needs enough technical definition to obtain comparable quotations, manufacture the part and verify what was delivered.

What has to be controlled before changing manufacturer?

Function, interfaces, material requirements, critical dimensions and fits, manufacturing constraints and inspection criteria. Without that baseline, two quotations may describe technically different outcomes.

When is a second source valuable?

When a single supplier creates unacceptable lead-time, price, continuity or obsolescence risk and the component can be technically defined well enough for controlled sourcing.

What should the owner keep?

The engineering package and verification logic required to source the same function again, rather than becoming dependent on the next manufacturer.
