Reverse Engineering and Manufacturing Localization

Structured workflow from a physical sample and feasibility check to design documentation, RFQ, cost model, prototype, pilot batch and repeatable production.

From a physical sample to controlled production

The dataset treats reverse engineering as recovery of reproducible engineering knowledge, not geometry capture alone. The goal is to reduce dependency on a single supplier and make the solution manufacturable and verifiable.

The Russian and English pages refer to the same dataset identity: https://pavel-samuta.github.io/engineering-data/reverse-engineering-localization/#dataset.
