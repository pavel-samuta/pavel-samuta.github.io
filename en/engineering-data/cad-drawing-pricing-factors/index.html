CAD Drawing and 3D Model Pricing Factors

Structured factors affecting the effort and cost of mechanical drawings and 3D models: complexity, information density, accuracy, manufacturability, reverse engineering, calculations, deadlines and changes.

Price follows engineering uncertainty

The dataset separates historical price examples from the underlying factors that drive engineering effort. Historical values are not presented as current commercial rates.

The Russian and English pages refer to the same dataset identity: https://pavel-samuta.github.io/engineering-data/cad-drawing-pricing-factors/#dataset.
