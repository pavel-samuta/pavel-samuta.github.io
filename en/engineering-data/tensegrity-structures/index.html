Tensegrity Structures

Structured engineering data on compression members, tension elements, prestress, self-equilibrium, stability, modularity, failure modes, history and applications.

Tension and compression form one system

The dataset describes tensegrity as a structural system in which discontinuous compression members interact with a continuous tension network, including stability, failure and modular behavior.

The Russian and English pages refer to the same dataset identity: https://pavel-samuta.github.io/engineering-data/tensegrity-structures/#dataset.
