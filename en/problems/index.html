INDUSTRIAL PROBLEM MAP

Start with the problem, not the service name

I step in when engineering uncertainty is already turning into downtime, supplier dependency, rework, delayed commissioning or unnecessary manufacturing cost.

The OEM no longer supplies a critical part

Obsolete and discontinued spare parts, unacceptable lead times, legacy equipment and no practical replacement route.

We have the physical part, but no usable manufacturing data

Recover geometry, fits, material requirements and manufacturing documentation from a sample and its operating context.

The drawings are missing, incomplete or no longer match the machine

Rebuild reliable as-built technical documentation for maintenance, sourcing, modification and repeat manufacture.

The supplier has an answer, but the owner needs an independent technical position

Review specifications, interfaces, exclusions, documentation and acceptance criteria before contract commitment.

The machine is ready for FAT

Turn a supplier demonstration into traceable evidence against measurable requirements before shipment or final payment.

Keep, modernize or replace legacy equipment?

Compare remaining function, obsolescence, maintainability, interfaces, downtime and capital implications.

The part works, but it is unnecessarily expensive to manufacture

Find cost locked into geometry, setups, tolerances, welding, machining, inspection and non-standard components.

We need a second manufacturing source

Convert supplier-dependent knowledge into controlled specifications, drawings and verification requirements.
